# Sources

* H. Koshita, *An example of relations on the Ext-quiver for the Suzuki
  group Sz(8) in characteristic 2*, Journal of Symbolic Computation 42
  (2007), 429-442. The global quiver and 23 relations are Theorem 1.
* G. J. A. Schneider, *The structure of the projective indecomposable
  modules of the Suzuki group Sz(8) in characteristic 2*, Mathematics of
  Computation 60 (1993), 779-786. The local normalizer and projective-module
  data are used as background checks.
* E. Green and O. Solberg, QPA: Quivers and Path Algebras,
  https://gap-packages.github.io/qpa/.
