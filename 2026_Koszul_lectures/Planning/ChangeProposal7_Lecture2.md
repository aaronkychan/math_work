We now consider algebras whose ideal of relations is generated in degree $2$.

\begin{definition}\label{def:quadratic}
A graded algebra $A$ is \defn{quadratic} if there exist a finite-dimensional semisimple $\Bbbk$-algebra $S$, a finite-dimensional $S$-bimodule $V$, and an $S$-sub-bimodule $R\subset V\otimes_S V$ such that $A\cong T_S(V)/(R)$ as graded algebras, where $V$ is in degree $1$ and $(R)$ is the two-sided ideal of $T_S(V)$ generated by $R$.
\end{definition}

An isomorphism $A\cong T_S(V)/(R)$ as in Definition~\ref{def:quadratic} is called a \defn{quadratic presentation} of $A$.
